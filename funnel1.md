# Restaurant AI Receptionist Sales Funnel

Website: restaurantaireceptinist.com

## Goal

Build a simple, high-converting sales funnel around one core restaurant-owner problem:

> Missed calls = missed revenue.

The website should focus less on explaining AI technology and more on helping restaurant owners quickly understand the business value, hear the product work, estimate ROI, and start a trial or book a demo.

---

## 1. Core Positioning

Primary message:

**Never lose another reservation because nobody answered the phone.**

Alternative positioning:

- Your restaurant is busy. Your phone receptionist does not have to be.
- AI answers your calls, handles reservations, answers common questions, and routes important calls 24/7.
- Stop letting missed calls turn into missed revenue.

Avoid leading with technical AI language. Lead with restaurant outcomes.

---

## 2. Hero Section

### Headline

**Never Miss Another Restaurant Call**

### Subheadline

Your AI receptionist answers 24/7, handles reservations and common questions, and lets your staff focus on guests in front of them.

### Primary CTA

**Try the AI Receptionist**

### Secondary CTA

**Book a 15-Minute Demo**

The primary CTA should allow the visitor to hear or call a live AI receptionist whenever possible.

---

## 3. Live Demo Experience

The strongest conversion tool should be an immediate product demonstration.

Recommended flow:

1. Visitor lands on the website.
2. Visitor clicks **Try the AI Receptionist**.
3. Visitor calls or interacts with a live demo.
4. Demo behaves like a restaurant receptionist.
5. Visitor is prompted to create a version for their own restaurant.

Recommended follow-up CTA:

**Create My Restaurant Demo**

The website should demonstrate the product instead of forcing visitors to read long explanations.

---

## 4. ROI Calculator

Add a section titled:

## Your Unanswered Phone Is Costing You Money

Inputs:

- Missed calls per day
- Average reservation or order value
- Estimated percentage of missed callers who would have converted
- Number of operating days per month

Example:

- Missed calls per day: 8
- Average value: $40
- Conversion rate: 40%
- Operating days: 30

Estimated monthly revenue at risk:

**$3,840**

Then compare the estimate to the monthly cost of the service.

Example copy:

> Recover only a few missed calls each month and the receptionist can pay for itself.

Clearly label ROI calculations as estimates based on customer-provided assumptions.

## 13. Sales Call Flow

The sales call should diagnose the restaurant's problem before presenting features.

Ask:

1. How many calls do you receive?
2. When are calls most likely to be missed?
3. What are callers usually asking?
4. Do you take reservations over the phone?
5. Do callers place takeout orders?
6. What reservation platform do you use?
7. What POS system do you use?
8. What happens when nobody answers?
9. How many staff members currently answer calls?
10. What would solving missed calls be worth to the restaurant?

Then demonstrate the customer's exact situation.

Example sales framing:

> You mentioned Friday and Saturday dinner service causes roughly 10 unanswered calls. Here is how the receptionist would handle those calls.

## 16. Conversion Metrics

Track the complete funnel.

Core metrics:

- Website visitors
- Visitor → live demo rate
- Visitor → lead rate
- Lead → booked demo rate
- Booked demo → attendance rate
- Demo → trial rate
- Trial → paid conversion rate
- Customer acquisition cost
- Monthly recurring revenue
- Average revenue per customer
- Churn
- Customer lifetime value
- Calls answered
- Reservations captured

Example:

```text
1,000 visitors
↓
150 try the demo
↓
80 become leads
↓
35 book a meeting
↓
25 attend
↓
10 become customers
```

At $299/month:

```text
10 customers × $299 = $2,990 new MRR
```

Use the funnel metrics to identify the true bottleneck.

Examples:

- High traffic + low demo usage = landing-page problem
- High demo usage + low lead rate = offer problem
- High booked-demo rate + low sales = sales/product-fit problem
- High trials + low paid conversion = onboarding/value problem
