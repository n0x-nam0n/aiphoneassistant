Before anything goes live

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Will Hostess replace my staff?

No. It handles overflow and repetitive calls so your team can stay with guests and make decisions that require judgment.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. Your existing number can ring your team first, then forward unanswered or after-hours calls.

Can it confirm reservations or events?

No. Hostess captures the request and tells callers that your team must confirm availability, pricing, and details.

What happens when it does not know?

It sticks to approved information and captures a callback request or escalates to a person.

Does it replace my event CRM?

No. Hostess is the phone capture layer that feeds the process your events team already uses.

Are calls recorded?

Recording is off by default. Any recording requires an approved policy and appropriate disclosure.
