What is a restaurant AI receptionist?

Hostess is a managed AI receptionist for restaurants. It answers missed, overflow and after-hours calls using information your restaurant approves, captures requests and transfers calls that need a person.

Can Hostess confirm reservations?

The initial service captures reservation requests and marks them as pending review. It does not confirm a reservation without authorization from your team or a connected reservation system.

Will it replace restaurant employees?

No. Hostess handles overflow and repetitive calls while your staff stays available for guests and decisions requiring human judgment.

What if it does not know an answer?

It is restricted to approved information and transfers or takes a message when uncertain. Allergens, safety incidents and payment disputes always go to a person.

How much does it cost?

The Founding Restaurant Pilot is $500 setup plus $299 per month for 90 days. The Managed Restaurant Receptionist plan is $1,500 setup plus $599 per month. Integrations are scoped separately.

Can we start with after-hours calls?

Yes. A focused pilot can begin with unanswered, overflow and after-hours calls while your existing number and daytime workflow stay in place.
